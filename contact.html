Frequently asked questions

Most questions are answered below. Close this note to see the list.
Is fuel included?
The city plan includes 80 km of fuel. For outstation bookings you pay for the fuel you use; the car leaves with a full tank.
Can I take the car to another state?
Yes, with prior permission. An interstate permit charge of ₹500 applies and the driver must be on the rental agreement.
What if the car breaks down?
Call the number on the key tag. Roadside assistance reaches you anywhere in Gujarat, and a replacement car is arranged if the repair takes more than four hours.
