About Drive Easy

A small Jamnagar business that believes a rental car should be clean, full and on time.

  1. Home
  2. About

Our story

Drive Easy started in 2024 with three hatchbacks parked behind a small office on Airport Road. The idea was simple: people in Jamnagar should be able to rent a car without arguing about the meter, the fuel or the driver’s schedule.

Today the fleet has hatchbacks, sedans and an automatic, and every car is serviced in house. We still hand over the keys ourselves, and we still photograph the odometer with you before you drive away.

  • Locally owned, locally operated
  • No fuel markup, no hidden charges
  • 24x7 roadside assistance in Gujarat

How we work

  • Booking
  • Handover
  • Safety

Book online or on the phone. You get a confirmation with the car number, pickup time and the fare, and you can change the dates free of cost up to four hours before pickup.

At handover we check the licence, photograph the odometer and fuel gauge with you, and explain the controls. The car leaves with a full tank.

Every car carries comprehensive insurance, a first aid kit, a spare tyre and a fire extinguisher. Service is done every 5,000 km in our own workshop.
